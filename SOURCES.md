# Original asset sources

All selected artwork and animation files are by Kay Lousberg (KayKit), distributed in these free packs under CC0 1.0. Original license texts are preserved in licenses/.

- Characters and accessories: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0
- Terrain, buildings and camp props: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Medieval-Hexagon-Pack-1.0
- Furniture: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Furniture-Bits-1.0
- Space base modules: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Space-Base-Bits-1.0
- Dedicated character/tool animations: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-character-animations
- Forest: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-forest
- Tools: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/rpg-tools-bits
- Resources: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/resource-bits

The GitHub packs were obtained from the author's own GitHub account; the other packs were downloaded through the author's official itch.io free download flow. No paid tiers were accessed.

Modifications: packed glTF buffers and images into self-contained GLB; combined same-rig animation channels by exact node name; generated smaller animation subsets; assembled three new scene layouts from the assets. These modifications do not imply authorship of the original artwork.

Three.js (MIT): https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js . Bundled runtime, GLTFLoader and OrbitControls are included; license in vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.

Clean survival character editions also remove the preset weapon mesh references from their default scenes; body/cosmetic meshes, skinning and rig are retained. Original character GLBs and separate accessory models are preserved.
